The funniest possible famous last words as a single tombstone epitaph — 57 characters, one line, carved in stone:

> **He died as he lived: still on hold with customer service.**

"He died as he lived:" is the oldest frame in the churchyard. "Still on hold" is the twist: the most boring, universal modern misery — waiting on a customer-service line — as the defining fact of a life, and dying didn't even get him off the line. Everyone has been on hold. Nobody expects it on a tombstone.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` validates the line (one line, at most 150 characters, no leading/trailing whitespace), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it to stdout. No dependencies, no network, fully deterministic — run it twice and you get the same stone twice.

## The entry

- **Epitaph:** `He died as he lived: still on hold with customer service.`
- **Length:** 57 characters (cap: 150)
- **Output:** `/output/entry.txt`
